        Start here

        • The pink numbers mark each feature. Hover over a number to read what it does. Press ① Numbers (top right) to hide them, and press it again to bring them back.
        • Take the tour (top right) lights up each feature in turn and opens the menus for you. Use Next and Back. End tour or Esc stops it at any time.
        • This Help panel has the full detail. Type a word in the search box and press Enter to jump to the first match, or click a heading in the contents above.
        • To close Help, press Close (top of this panel) or Esc. Narrow and Half change its width, or you can drag its yellow left edge.

        Where things are now

        • Top bar: the Jim's Daybook title, the search box (next to the player), then ⛶ Full screen, Take the tour, ① Numbers and ? Help.
        • Main menu (the coloured row, BBC-style): Home and your categories. Long names are shortened, and hovering over one shows its full name.
        • Bright menus next to it: 🧵 Threads, 🌱 month (garden), 🔎 Topics, 🏷 Tags and ☰ My Daybook. Click one to open it. Click it again, click anywhere outside it, or press Esc to close it.
        • ☰ My Daybook holds Watch later, Moments, Channel library and subscriptions, Manage tags, Settings and backups, Feedback for Claude, Note for Claude, and your most-watched channels.
        • The player fills the left. Under it are the title and the action buttons (☆ Moment, 🧵 Follow this, ⏱ Later and so on).
        • The panel beside the player has Details, Chapters, Transcript, Links & resources and a 💬 Comments switch.
        • New videos is under the player. Subscriptions is the wall of big channel logos at the bottom: every channel in your library, newest uploads first.
        • The old headline page from stage 2 is gone. Everything it did is now in the menus above. The stage 1 page is kept as daybook-classic.html, with a link at the bottom of ⚙ Settings.

        The Daybook helper

        • The helper is a small private program on your laptop. It lets videos play inside the Daybook, fetches transcripts and comments, reads pasted links and saves your channel and topic changes. Only this laptop can reach it.
        • Always open the Daybook from your bookmark: http://127.0.0.1:8765. If you open the daybook.html file directly, videos open in YouTube or FreeTube instead, and the transcripts, comments, pasted links and YouTube suggestions don't work.
        • The helper starts by itself when the laptop starts. You don't need to do anything day to day.
        • If the bookmark won't load, or Claude has changed the helper, restart it. Open Konsole, paste this with Ctrl+Shift+V, and press Enter: systemctl --user restart daybook-helper.service. Then press Ctrl+Shift+R in the Daybook tab. It's also in Jim's Toolbox.
        • To install it again (for example after reinstalling Linux), paste this into Konsole: bash "/mnt/STORAGE/CLAUDE AI PROJECTS/Daybook/install_helper.sh".
        • Transcripts and comments come from yt-dlp, which is already installed. If they stop working for every video, yt-dlp probably needs updating, because YouTube changes things now and then. Ask Claude for the update line.
        • The evening fetch is separate from the helper. It still runs at about 17:30 and brings in new videos.

        Watching a video

        • Click the picture in the player to play the video inside the Daybook. Double-click the video for full screen, and press Esc to come back.
        • Clicks inside the video can't open YouTube. Use the ↗ buttons when you want to leave.
        • ☆ Moment saves the current second as a moment (see Moments).
        • 🧵 Follow this builds a topic thread from this video and opens 🧵 Threads. Once you've followed it, the button shows 🧵 Following.
        • ⏱ Later puts it on your Watch later shelf. ✓ Watched ticks it off (press again to undo). ✕ Not for me takes it off New videos. The video stays in the player either way, so you can finish watching it.
        • 🔊 reads the title and description aloud.
        • ↗ YouTube and ↗ FreeTube open the video there, from the point you've reached, and pause the Daybook's player.
        • 📺 is a reminder of how to cast to the TV (see Casting to the TV).
        • A few channels (for example Sky Sports Premier League) don't let their videos play inside other pages. For those, the player shows Open in YouTube and Open in FreeTube buttons instead.

        The panel: details, chapters, transcript, links, comments

        • Details: the description, the upload date, the length, the category and any tags. A ★ note marks a short video on one of your AI Field Guide subjects.
        • Chapters (the number says how many): click a time to jump the video there. ☆ beside a chapter saves it as a moment, and turns to ★. Press ★ to remove it.
        • Transcript: the captions, fetched when you open the tab (a few seconds). Click any time to jump there. 🔊 Copy for reading copies it (or just the part you highlight first); then press Meta+Alt+C to hear it in your own voice (Meta+Alt+X stops). If it fails, press Try again.
        • Links & resources: every web link from the video's full description. The nightly fetch saves them; if a video has none saved, the Daybook helper reads the full description (a few seconds, only this laptop). Links open in a new tab, and the linked sites are only visited when you click.
        • 💬 Comments is a switch. Turn it on to read the comments (10 to 20 seconds to fetch). It's read-only: nothing is posted, liked or signed in. Click any tab to switch comments off again.

        Home, categories and New videos

        • The numbers on Home and each category count the videos from the last fortnight (or the days you chose in Settings), the same ones New videos shows. Videos you've set to None ⊘ (through a tag) aren't counted.
        • Show older videos (on the New videos line) adds the older ones the Daybook still holds, about two months' worth, and the numbers grow to match. Only the last fortnight goes back.
        • Bigger list: when there are lots of videos, drag the line beside the date up (the ⇕ tab), click it, or press ⤢ Bigger list. New videos then fills the space where the player was. Click a video and the player comes back with it ready. To go back without picking one, drag the line down, click it again, press ▣ Back to the player, or press Esc.
        • How many days New videos shows: the usual is the last fortnight. Change it in ☰ My Daybook → Settings and backups → New videos shows (3 days, a week, a fortnight, 30 days or everything). The numbers on the main menu follow it.
        • Clearing videos you don't want: hover over a card and press its ✕ (top right). It's the same as ✕ Not for me: the video leaves New videos. Press Undo in the message that pops up if you didn't mean it, or find it later in ☰ My Daybook → Watch later → Not for me → Put back.
        • How long videos stay: the evening fetch keeps videos for 60 days, then they drop out by themselves. Only the days you chose show in New videos, so older ones don't clutter it. Anything you've saved stays: Watch later, Moments, Threads and your Watched list are kept in this browser, and they still play in the Daybook's player (without the picture or chapters once the video has gone from the feed).
        • Home keeps New videos folded away until you click a channel on the Subscriptions wall, so the wall gets the room.
        • Pick a category and New videos shows all its videos from the last fortnight. Click a channel logo on the Subscriptions wall to narrow it to one channel. Click the same logo again to go back to all of them.
        • When you pick a channel, a row with its large logo shows how many new videos it has. ● LIVE will mark live videos once the evening fetch records them.
        • On the cards, NEW means it arrived since your last visit, FG means a short AI Field Guide video, and ADDED means you pasted it in yourself. Cards you've watched fade. The one in the player is outlined.
        • Click a card to put it in the player. Then click the picture to play it.
        • The Subscriptions logos are ringed in each logo's main colour. The small number counts its new videos. Faded logos have nothing new in that time; they come back to full colour when you hover.
        • Wall and feed: the evening fetch reads every channel in your library, so all of them show on the wall. Only channels marked IN FEED (15 per category at most) count towards Home and the category numbers. Channels still in Unsorted show on the wall too.
        • For more of the wall, press ⤢ Bigger list (it hides the player), or Full screen.
        • New videos arrive each evening at about 17:30. Reload the page to see them.

        Search and suggestions

        • Type in the search box to find channels 📺, tags 🏷, videos ▶ and chapters ⏱ in your Daybook. Use the arrow keys and Enter, or click one.
        • Picking a channel shows its new videos. Picking a tag narrows to that tag. Picking a video puts it in the player. Picking a chapter plays the video from that chapter.
        • The ▾ filter on the right of the box chooses where suggestions come from. Daybook only is private: nothing leaves the laptop. Daybook + YouTube also asks YouTube for suggestions as you type, so YouTube sees the words you type (not who you are).
        • With Daybook + YouTube, the 🌐 suggestions search YouTube in a new tab. Pressing Enter with nothing picked does the same.
        • If YouTube sends nothing back, the list says so. Try restarting the helper (see The Daybook helper).
        • Help has its own search: the box at the top of this panel.

        Paste a YouTube link

        1. Copy a YouTube link (a video, a Short or a youtu.be link) and paste it into the search box with Ctrl+V.
        2. A panel opens with the video's picture, title and channel after a few seconds.
        3. Choose a Category, or press + New category, then type a name and pick a colour.
        4. Choose a Tag, or press + New tag and type one. The Daybook makes a guess, which you can change.
        5. Tick what you want: ⏱ Watch later, 📺 Add the channel to my Daybook (switched on) and ▶ Play now.
        6. Press Save. The video joins New videos with an ADDED label. Cancel closes the panel without saving anything.
        7. If you added the channel, it reaches tonight's fetch by itself. With the helper running, the channel list is saved straight to Downloads.

        Topic threads

        • Press 🧵 Follow this under the player. The Daybook gathers every video in your feed on the same topic, from any channel or category, and opens 🧵 Threads.
        • It lays them out as a watch path: one anchor to watch in full (it prefers 15 to 60 minutes), then up to three others to watch or skim.
        • For the follow-up videos it shows only the chapters on your topic, in bold. Chapters that repeat the anchor, intros, sponsors and wrap-ups are greyed with the reason. Show all chapters shows the rest.
        • Thread words are what the thread matches on. Remove a word with ×, or type a new one and press Enter. The path rebuilds straight away.
        • Type over the thread's name to rename it. The name is also used for Search YouTube for this topic.
        • Not in this thread takes a video out. ✓ Watched ticks it off but keeps it. Also on this topic lists further matches.
        • ☆ Moments from this thread lists the moments you saved from its videos.
        • You can follow several topics: each one has a tab. Delete thread (press twice) removes one.
        • Threads are worked out on this laptop. Nothing is sent anywhere.

        Moments

        • Save a moment with ☆ Moment under the player (the current second) or ☆ beside a chapter.
        • Open them from ☰ My Daybook → Moments. The number says how many you have.
        • New moments wait in the Inbox. Moments saved inside a thread go into a topic named after it. Use the menu beside a moment to give it a topic, Move all for a whole video, or New topic….
        • Click a moment's time to play from there. Type in its box to add a note. × deletes it.
        • Open a topic and press Export to notebook. That saves a zip to Downloads, one note per video with its picture and every moment linked to the exact second, ready to import into UpNote.
        • Exported moments are marked Filed and hidden. Tick Show filed to see them, use Unfile to bring one back, or press Remove filed moments twice once UpNote has them.
        • Pictures are only included while the video is still in your feed (about 60 days), so export a topic before then.

        Tags and Manage tags

        • Tags say what a video is about. Each one matches on a list of words in a video's title, description or chapters. A video can have several.
        • Open 🏷 Tags in the menu bar to narrow New videos to one tag. The button then shows the tag's name. Untagged shows the videos no tag caught.
        • With a tag picked, its menu offers More ★ (top of the list), Less ↓ (bottom) and None ⊘ (hidden unless you pick the tag). Press the same button again to undo. Go deeper offers narrower tags, and Next door offers related ones.
        • ☰ My Daybook → Manage tags opens the tag manager. There you can add a tag (name, category or Any category, and optionally a tag to sit under), rename one, change the words it Matches on, move it Under another, Merge into another, or Delete it (press twice).
        • Suggested from videos you've opened lists shared words that no tag covers yet. Use Add as tag or Ignore.

        Your topics and Beyond your channels

        • Both are in the 🔎 Topics menu.
        • Your topics are your own subjects, saved as YouTube searches. Type one, choose a category and press Add. Each evening the fetch keeps up to 6 videos from the last month for each topic.
        • Beyond your channels shows fresh videos on trends around your interests, from channels you don't follow. + Library files a channel you like under Unsorted, switched off. Edit trend searches changes what's looked for.
        • After changing either list, press Save topics for tonight. With the helper running, it saves straight to Downloads, and the evening fetch picks it up.
        • Ask Claude for ideas is optional: copy the message into a Claude chat, paste the reply back and press Add these.

        Garden this month

        • How UK gardeners are coping with the season lists your garden channels' recent videos about the weather. Two views pairs two of your channels on the same subject.

        Managing subscriptions

        • Open ☰ My Daybook → Channel library and subscriptions. It lists every channel by category, plus Unsorted for ones not filed yet. Click a category's name to fold it away.
        • Add one channel: paste a link to any of its videos into the search box, tick 📺 Add the channel to my Daybook and press Save (see Paste a YouTube link).
        • Bring in your old YouTube subscriptions: press Import subscriptions. It takes a Google Takeout subscriptions.csv, a FreeTube or NewPipe export, or a pasted list of channel names or links (one per line). Imported channels arrive in Unsorted, switched off.
        • Filing from Unsorted: drag a channel onto a category, or use its Move… menu. Little and often is fine.
        • In or out of the feed: × takes a channel out of the evening feed and + puts it back. Each category can have up to 15 channels switched on.
        • Delete: press 🗑 Delete channels, then press a channel's bin twice. Done deleting hides the bins.
        • Always finish with Save channel changes. With the helper running, it saves daybook-channels.json straight to Downloads, and tonight's fetch picks it up. Changes don't reach the fetch until you save.

        Google Takeout: getting subscriptions.csv

        1. In Brave, go to takeout.google.com and sign in to the Google account your YouTube subscriptions are on.
        2. Press Deselect all near the top of the list.
        3. Scroll down to YouTube and YouTube Music and tick it.
        4. Press its All YouTube data included button. Untick everything except subscriptions, then press OK.
        5. Scroll to the bottom and press Next step. Leave it as "Send download link via email", "Export once" and .zip, then press Create export.
        6. When Google's email arrives (usually within minutes for subscriptions), download the zip and open it. The file is in Takeout → YouTube and YouTube Music → subscriptions → subscriptions.csv.
        7. In the Daybook, press Import subscriptions, choose that file, then file the channels from Unsorted and press Save channel changes.
        8. To back out: the import only adds channels to Unsorted, switched off. Delete any you don't want and save.
        9. Only import subscriptions.csv. Takeout also gives playlist files (Watch later, music playlists) that list videos, not channels. The Daybook now refuses those, and tidies away any video entries a playlist file left in Unsorted the next time the page opens.

        FreeTube, NewPipe or a pasted list

        • FreeTube: Settings → Data Settings → Export Subscriptions, choosing the FreeTube format. Then import that file in the Daybook.
        • NewPipe (Android): Subscriptions tab → ⋮ menu → Export to → File. Copy the file to the laptop (USB or your usual way), then import it.
        • Pasted list: in Import subscriptions, paste channel names or channel links, one per line, and press Import.

        • Open videos in (on this device): YouTube, the FreeTube app, the PipePipe app or an Invidious site. Each device keeps its own choice. Android starts on PipePipe, so tapping a video opens it there straight away. This is used when the helper isn't running and for links outside the player. Tick "just for now" to change it only until the tab closes.

        Full screen

        • ⛶ Full screen (top right) fills the whole screen with the Daybook and hides Brave's tabs. Press it again (it now says Exit full screen) or press Esc to come back. F11 does the same.
        • Double-clicking the video makes just the video full screen. Esc comes back.

        Casting to the TV

        • Once only: in Brave, open brave://settings/extensions and turn on Media Router, then restart Brave.
        • To cast: open Brave's menu ☰ → Cast… (in some versions it's under "Save and share" or "More tools"). Choose Sources → Cast tab, then pick the NVIDIA Shield.
        • The whole Daybook tab appears on the TV. Press ⛶ Full screen, or double-click the video, so the TV shows just the video.
        • To stop: open ☰ → Cast… again and press Stop casting, or press the blue cast icon in Brave's toolbar.
        • If the Shield doesn't appear: the laptop and the Shield must be on the same Wi-Fi. With NordVPN on the Shield, open NordVPN → Settings and turn Local network discovery on. With Proton VPN on a paid plan, LAN connections are allowed by themselves.

        Brave's media controls

        • While a video plays, a media button appears in Brave's toolbar. It gives you play and pause from any tab, and picture-in-picture (a small floating video window you can drag around while you work in other tabs).
        • In recent Brave versions it can also save the current frame as a picture.
        • To close picture-in-picture, press × on the small window, or "back to tab" to return it to the Daybook.

        Opening videos in YouTube or FreeTube

        • ↗ YouTube opens the video in a new Brave tab. ↗ FreeTube opens it in the FreeTube app. Both start from the point you've reached and pause the Daybook's player.
        • On Android, ↗ PipePipe takes the place of ↗ FreeTube. On the Daybook's own web address (jimw137.github.io/jd-feed-4k7q/), one tap opens the video in PipePipe. If PipePipe doesn't open, or you're using the old Claude page, the video's link is copied instead: open PipePipe, tap 🔍 and Paste. No YouTube page is opened.
        • Use them for channels that block playing inside other pages, or when you want YouTube's own features.
        • To return, close that tab or the FreeTube window. The Daybook tab is still where you left it.

        If something doesn't work

        • A change from Claude doesn't show: press Ctrl+Shift+R in the Daybook tab (reload without Brave's stored copy).
        • Videos open in YouTube instead of playing here, or transcripts, comments and pasted links say they need the helper: check the address bar says 127.0.0.1:8765. If the bookmark won't load, restart the helper (see The Daybook helper).
        • One channel won't play inside the Daybook: that channel blocks it. Use Open in YouTube or Open in FreeTube.
        • No new videos today: the fetch runs at about 17:30 and keeps the previous videos if the laptop was offline. Its report is fetch_report.txt in the Daybook folder.
        • Channel or topic changes didn't arrive: press Save channel changes or Save topics for tonight. The fetch only sees saved changes.
        • Something else: write a Note for Claude and paste Feedback for Claude into your next chat.

        Privacy

        • The page itself loads nothing from the internet. Icons and pictures are local copies made by the evening fetch.
        • YouTube is only contacted when you choose: pressing play (using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie player, with no account), opening a transcript or comments (fetched by yt-dlp through the helper), pasting a link, or choosing Daybook + YouTube suggestions.
        • Nothing is ever posted, liked or signed in. Your notes, moments, tags, topics and settings stay in this browser on this laptop.
        • Topic and trend searches are run by the evening fetch as ordinary YouTube searches without an account. YouTube sees the search words, not who you are.
